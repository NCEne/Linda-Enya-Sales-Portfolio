01 — The approach

Sales, taught — not sold.

My background in teaching wired me to break down complex problems, listen first, and prioritize real understanding over a script. I use data and trigger-based signals to find the right doors — then use empathy and sharp communication to actually open them. I'm not chasing a pitch; I'm rescuing prospects from automated noise and helping them see the solution clearly enough to say yes.
